Membird Support

We’re here to help with purchases, restoring access, lessons, or technical problems.

Contact support

Please email safakcvs@gmail.com with the subject “Membird support”. Do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

For a purchase issue, include the product purchased, the approximate purchase date, and a short description of what happened. Apple handles payment processing, so we may ask you to contact Apple Support for billing issues.
